Sample source & required positions

Built-in source: Wavebase Stratocaster — real electric-guitar notes recorded without effects and released to the public domain. The low string in the source session was Drop-D, so its E/F/G recordings come from slightly higher physical frets, but the pitches and string register are correct.

Your own files still work: S1_F0_V1.wav. S1 = low E and S6 = high E; F is the fret; V is an optional alternate take.

Internet is needed to fetch the built-in WAVs after a page reload. Once loaded, they stay cached in memory for the session. If the remote library cannot load, the app falls back to the physical string model or any WAVs you supply.
